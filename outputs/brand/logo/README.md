## Concept

The mark uses a custom PPG monogram crossed by one continuous rising negative-space channel. The line represents the repeated stand–flick–mark progression without using a literal pen, ball, club, flag, course, or product diagram. The short rust terminal supplies a restrained landing accent. The public wordmark remains `Pen Pad Golf`.

Palette:

- Ink: `#193225`
- Paper: `#F7F1E4`
- Rust accent: `#9C3F2C`

## Production prompt

Create an original Pen Pad Golf identity on a warm paper background. Build a compact custom `PPG` Flickline monogram from two geometric P modules followed by an open G. Join the three forms with one continuous shallow rising paper-colored negative-space channel through their counters, terminating at a short inward G terminal. Center the exact title-case wordmark `Pen Pad Golf` below. Use a crisp flat vector style, dark ink green `#193225`, warm paper `#F7F1E4`, and at most one small rust `#9C3F2C` terminal accent. Keep the square composition legible at 40 px and inside an Instagram circular crop. Avoid gradients, shadows, mockups, extra text, golf balls, clubs, tees, flags, holes, scorecards, crests, seals, literal pens, hands, product diagrams, tournament styling, and imitation of established brands.

The built-in image-generation workflow produced the raster master. The failed dark transparent draft was rejected and is not part of the project deliverables.
