## Carousel sequence

1. Hook — “A whole round of golf starts with a pen.” Novel overhead watercolor scene of a hand, navy pen, paper course, water, bunker, trees, and red flag, plus the exact Flickline compact mark.
2. Step one — “Start in the tee box.” Novel close watercolor view of one navy pen standing upright in the tee box with the course leading to the flag.
3. Step two — “Flick toward the hole.” Novel action illustration of one believable fingertip flicking the upright pen along the course.
4. Steps three and four — “Mark the landing.” Novel overhead landing scene with one resting pen, one landing mark, and a dotted course path.
5. Finish — “Lowest score wins.” Novel course-border illustration around a calm center panel containing the exact Flickline primary lockup, CTA, and visible “In development” disclosure.

Every slide is 1080 × 1350 px. Critical text and marks stay inside feed crop-safe margins.
