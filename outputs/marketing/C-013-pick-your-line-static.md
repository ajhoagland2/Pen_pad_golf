## Purpose

Create a consideration-stage Instagram carousel that makes the printed course itself the visual hook. The package invites the viewer to imagine a line from tee box to flag while staying inside approved gameplay facts and the in-development boundary.

## Instagram-ready caption

Which line would you take? ⛳

Start in the tee box. Stand the pen upright, flick toward the hole, and mark where it lands as one stroke. Continue from the new mark until you reach the hole.

Pick your line. Count every landing. Lowest score wins.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. See how to play.

CTA: See how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment
