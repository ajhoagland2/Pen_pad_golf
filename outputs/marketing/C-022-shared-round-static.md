# C-022 — Shared round

Status: Brand Approved; founder publication approval Pending

## Purpose

Create a distinct, saveable Instagram carousel that turns the approved lowest-score mechanic into a shared-round invitation. It uses founder-owned Course 05 art and the unchanged Flickline identity without adding availability, product, performance, player-count, or pricing claims.

## Instagram-ready caption

Same printed course. Your own line. ⛳

Take turns with the pen. Start in the tee box, stand it upright, flick toward the hole, and mark every landing as one stroke. Continue from each mark until you reach the flag.

Then compare the scorecards. Lowest score wins.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. Save this for a shared round, then see how to play.

CTA: Save for a shared round and see how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Alt text

Four-slide cream-paper Pen Pad Golf carousel using founder-owned watercolor Course 05 art. Slide 1 says “Pass the pen. Share the round” beside the printed course. Slide 2 shows Player A’s rust landing trail and a complete upright navy pen anchored at the printed tee-box marker. Slide 3 shows a different gold landing trail for Player B on the same course. Slide 4 presents two blank scorecards, states that every landing counts as one stroke and lowest score wins, and includes the official primary Flickline lockup, save CTA, and in-development disclosure.
