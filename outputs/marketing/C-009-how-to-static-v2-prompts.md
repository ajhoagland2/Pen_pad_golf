# C-009 v2 image-generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation with three reference images: the approved cover hero, the owned rules artwork, and Classic Course 01.

## Shared visual system

Create novel Pen Pad Golf campaign illustrations rather than copies of the references. Use hand-painted watercolor and gouache, fine ink contours, tactile cream paper grain, vintage field-guide or game-manual character, and warm editorial light. Keep the palette to deep forest green, sage and olive fairways, navy pen details, rust-red flags, restrained gold, blue water, and natural wood. Preserve the physical premise of a pen played on a printed paper golf course. Use one clear focal action per frame. Do not generate logos, legible text, letters, numbers, watermarks, golf balls, golf clubs, plastic mini-golf, glossy 3D rendering, neon colors, duplicated pens, malformed hands, or impossible physics.

## Slide 1 — Hook

Vertical 4:5 overhead tabletop hero. Show one navy retractable pen about to begin a round on a cream paper pad, one believable adult hand entering from the lower-left, a winding course, water, bunker, evergreen clusters, and one red flag. Concentrate the course scene in the upper two-thirds and leave a broad dark-green, low-detail lower zone for deterministic headline copy.

## Slide 2 — Start

Wide 1.9:1 editorial crop. Show one navy retractable pen standing vertically on its tip inside a small illustrated tee box. Let the fairway curl toward one distant red flag, with small shrubs, water, and a restrained bunker. Keep the setup instantly readable with quiet paper around it. No hand.

## Slide 3 — Flick

Wide 1.9:1 action crop. Show one believable adult fingertip giving one upright navy pen a sideways flick at the start of a winding paper fairway. Use restrained motion lines and blue-ink trajectory dots toward one red flag. The action must read as flicking a pen on paper, not throwing it.

## Slide 4 — Mark

Wide 1.9:1 top-down landing scene. Show one navy pen resting naturally on the fairway beside one crisp blue landing mark. A dotted path enters from the tee and leads toward that mark; one red flag waits farther up the course. Add paper-edge shadows, shrubs, water, and bunker details without clutter.

## Slide 5 — Finish

Vertical 4:5 finish background. Let a winding watercolor fairway arrive at one red flag and cup, with one navy pen resting near a completed dotted path. Frame the outside edges with restrained trees, bunker, and water motifs while keeping a generous calm cream-paper center for the official logo, headline, CTA, and disclosure to be added later.
