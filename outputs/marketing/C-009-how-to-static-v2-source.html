How to play

A whole round of golf starts with a pen.

Swipe for the four-step version.

1

Step one

Start in the tee box.

Stand the pen upright to begin the hole.

2

Step two

Flick toward the hole.

Give the upright pen a flick toward the hole.

3

Steps three + four

Mark the landing.

Count one stroke. Keep going from the new mark.

Finish the round

Lowest score wins.

A little golf. A lot of fun.
