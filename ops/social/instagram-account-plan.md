## Launch checklist

1. Create the account using founder-controlled recovery information.
2. Enable two-factor authentication.
3. Switch to a professional business account.
4. Add the approved name, bio, image, and website link.
5. Review privacy, mention, message, comment, and login-alert settings.
6. Connect approved scheduling or Meta Business tooling only after the founder reviews access.
7. After the founder confirms Meta Business Suite access, Marketing may prepare non-public drafts from exact Brand-approved static packages even while publish timing or conversion-path gates remain unresolved. Do not create, revise, schedule, or publish Reels, video, narration, motion, or audio.
8. Use the static foundation set in this order: C-009 v2 after its new Brand approval, C-008 Flickline identity launch, then either C-006 product facts or C-004 lowest-score challenge.
9. For each draft, confirm the selected account, exact export files, slide order, crop, caption, alt text, disclosure, CTA, and unique tagged link. Save as draft only; do not schedule, publish, boost, or add spend.
10. Brand performs in-app Meta draft QA and records approval or a specific correction. Publishing remains blocked until Brand review, Meta draft QA, founder publish approval, analytics, feedback path, and other release gates pass.
11. Record draft evidence and baseline insights in the `Instagram Account` and `Content Pipeline` Google Sheet tabs without storing credentials or private account data.
