## About draft

Pen Pad Golf turns a pen and a printed course into a quick round of golf you can play almost anywhere. The idea is simple: start at the tee, stand the pen upright, flick toward the hole, and mark every landing as one stroke. Lowest score wins. We are currently developing and playtesting the first edition.
