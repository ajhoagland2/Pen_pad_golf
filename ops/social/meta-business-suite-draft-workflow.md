# Meta Business Suite draft workflow

Purpose: turn exact Brand-approved Pen Pad Golf static packages into reviewable, non-public Instagram drafts without crossing the founder-controlled scheduling or publishing gate.

## Entry gate

- Confirm the Pen Pad Golf Meta account is the selected destination.
- Confirm `Brand review` is `Approved` for the exact export files being uploaded.
- Confirm the campaign package contains the final caption, alt text, disclosure, CTA, and unique tagged destination.
- Founder publish approval is not required to create a saved draft. It is required before scheduling or publishing.

## Marketing draft procedure

1. Open Meta Business Suite and select the confirmed Pen Pad Golf Instagram account.
2. Start an Instagram feed post and upload the exact approved PNGs in numeric order.
3. Confirm every crop preserves critical art, copy, logo, CTA, and the `In development` disclosure.
4. Paste the approved caption verbatim. Add the approved alt text where supported.
5. Confirm the unique tagged link against the campaign package. Do not substitute a local path or broken `/product.html` route.
6. Disable or avoid platform templates, filters, enhancements, music, stickers, generated copy, and boosting.
7. Save as a draft. Do not schedule or publish.
8. Record the draft date, account, campaign ID, non-secret draft reference, and visual evidence in `Content Pipeline`.

## Brand in-app QA

Brand reviews the saved draft—not only the source files—for account selection, slide order, crop, legibility, artwork fidelity, caption, alt text, disclosure, CTA, and destination. Record `Meta draft QA` as `Approved` or `Needs revision` with one concrete next action.

## Release boundary

A saved draft is not a scheduled or published post. Marketing and Brand must stop at the draft stage until all release gates pass and the founder authorizes scheduling or publishing. Never boost or commit spend without separate founder approval.
