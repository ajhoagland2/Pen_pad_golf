# Founder approval policy

## Agents may execute autonomously

- Research, analysis, drafting, and internal revisions
- Deterministic asset generation and validation
- Meta Business Suite and Etsy draft preparation
- Routine monitoring and evidence collection
- Approved, policy-compliant customer replies
- Reporting, reconciliation, and non-sensitive data hygiene
- Post-publication verification and issue creation

## Founder approval required

| Action | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Public publish or scheduling | Exact creative/copy, Brand decision, destination, timing, commit and fingerprints |
| Spending or purchasing | Amount, vendor, purpose, unit economics, and scope |
| Price or promotion change | Current and proposed price, effective window, and expected impact |
| Refund or service exception | Order reference, policy boundary, and proposed remedy without customer private data |
| New product claim or policy | Exact language, evidence, affected platforms, and Brand decision |
| Inventory commitment | Quantity, cost, supplier, fulfillment capacity, and downside |
| Account ownership or security | Founder performs identity, recovery, MFA, banking, and tax actions directly |

Approval is scoped to the exact commit, artifact hashes, external account, action, and timing recorded in the request. A material change invalidates the approval.

## Decision vocabulary

- `Pending` — no decision has been made.
- `Approved` — the exact requested action may proceed.
- `Changes requested` — revise and return with new evidence.
- `Declined` — stop the requested action.
- `Superseded` — replaced by a newer request.
- `Expired` — approval window elapsed before execution.

## Separation of duties

- The authoring agent cannot approve its own Brand or founder gate.
- Brand approval verifies representation and claims; it does not authorize publication or spend.
- Founder approval authorizes the controlled action; the executing agent must still verify prerequisites.
- Completion requires execution evidence, not merely an approval.
