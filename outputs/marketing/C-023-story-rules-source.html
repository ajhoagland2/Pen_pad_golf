How to play Pen Pad Golf.

Four simple moves from the tee box to the flag.

01

Start / stand

Start in the tee box.

Stand the pen upright before the first flick.

Flick / mark

Flick toward the hole.

Mark the landing as one stroke. Continue from each mark.

Finish the round

Lowest score wins.

Count the round

Every landing is one stroke.
