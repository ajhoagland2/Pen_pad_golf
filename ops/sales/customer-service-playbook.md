# PPG customer service and outreach playbook

## Routine replies the Sales Agent may send

- How the game works, using approved gameplay facts
- Current development or availability status
- Order-status facts visible in Etsy
- Published processing, shipping, return, and shop policies
- Requests for clarification that do not promise a refund, replacement, discount, delivery date, or policy exception

## Escalate before sending

- Refunds, cancellations, replacements, reships, discounts, or credits
- Missing, damaged, late, or incorrect orders when a remedy is not already approved
- Wholesale, retail, partnership, press, influencer, licensing, or custom-product terms
- Safety, legal, intellectual-property, privacy, tax, or regulatory questions
- Harassment, threats, chargebacks, fraud, or requests for personal or payment information

## Reply standard

Respond promptly, use the customer's name only when available in the source system, acknowledge the question, give the shortest accurate answer, set realistic expectations, and include one clear next step. Keep order communication inside Etsy Messages whenever possible.

## Outreach rules

Use only consented, relevant contacts and platform-permitted outreach. Never scrape email addresses, buy lists, mass-message Etsy users, offer incentives for positive reviews, or move Etsy purchases off-platform.
