# C-015 — First-hole ballot static carousel

Status: Brand-approved under BRD-030; founder publication approval Pending

## Purpose

Create a consideration-stage Instagram carousel that turns two founder-owned course illustrations into a simple first-hole choice. The package invites feedback without implying product availability, a finalized course count, or measured preference.

## Instagram-ready caption

First pick: Course A or Course B? ⛳

Both routes begin the same way: start in the tee box, stand the pen upright, and flick toward the hole. Mark each landing as one stroke. Lowest score wins.

Which printed hole would you play first?

Pen Pad Golf is in development. See how to play and share your pick.

CTA: See how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Carousel sequence

1. Ballot cover — “Which hole would you play first?” on deep green, with large A/B choice tokens and the exact compact Flickline mark.
2. Course A — one founder-owned illustrated course in a taped field-card frame, labeled “Find your line,” with the literal tee-box and flick instruction.
3. Course B — one different founder-owned illustrated course in the same ballot system, labeled “Choose your start,” with the literal landing/scoring rule.
4. Vote close — “Course A or Course B?” with two large ballot choices, the exact primary Flickline lockup, CTA, and visible development disclosure.

Every slide is 1080 × 1350 px. Critical copy and official identity remain inside feed crop-safe margins.
