## Purpose

Create a saveable, challenge-led Instagram carousel that turns the approved first-flick mechanic into a simple audience prompt. ## Instagram-ready caption

Where would your first flick land? ⛳

Start in the tee box. Stand the pen upright, flick toward the hole, and mark where it lands as one stroke. Continue from that mark until you reach the flag.

How many flicks would it take? Lowest score wins.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. Save the first-flick challenge, then see how to play.

CTA: Save the challenge and see how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Alt text

Four-slide cream-paper Pen Pad Golf carousel using founder-owned watercolor Course 04 art. Slide 1 asks where the first flick starts and shows the full course from tee box to flag. Slide 2 shows a complete upright navy pen, tip, and shadow inside the printed tee box. Slide 3 places a target marker on broad playable fairway and explains that the landing is one stroke and the next starting point. Slide 4 highlights the flag, asks how many flicks it would take, states that lowest score wins, and includes the official primary Flickline lockup, save CTA, and in-development disclosure.
