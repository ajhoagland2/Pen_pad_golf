## Objective

Turn the real Pen Pad Golf mechanic into a low-friction pre-launch challenge that sends 300 qualified visitors to the how-to-play experience and earns 15 verified feedback-intent conversions. Pen Pad Golf is in development; this package invites people to watch the demonstration and does not imply that a playable course or product is publicly available.

## Audience

Golfers, tabletop-game fans, gift shoppers, and tactile-game players who are likely to watch the demonstration and, if they have already playtested Pen Pad Golf, share useful feedback.

## Campaign proposition

**How low can you score with a pen?**

The challenge is simple: start in the tee box, stand the pen upright, flick toward the hole, and mark each landing as one stroke. Lowest score wins.
