# C-016 — The page is the course static carousel

Status: Ready for Brand review; founder publication approval Pending

## Purpose

Create an awareness-stage Instagram carousel that makes the printed page itself the visual hero. The close-up field-note system turns two founder-owned course illustrations into a literal tee-to-flag sequence without implying availability, adding product components, or introducing new gameplay claims.

The page is the course. ⛳

A little golf. A lot of fun.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. See how to play.

CTA: See how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Carousel sequence

1. Brand-led cover — “The page is the course.” over deep green with a circular close-up of founder-owned course art and the exact compact Flickline mark.
2. Tee-box close-up — a deterministic upright navy pen sits inside the visible printed tee box beside the literal start instruction.
3. Course-reading view — one printed course with a restrained route trace and the literal flick-and-mark scoring instruction.
4. Flag close-up — the exact primary Flickline lockup, approved master promise, lowest-score rule, CTA, and visible development disclosure.

Every slide is 1080 × 1350 px. Critical copy and official identity remain inside feed crop-safe margins. Unapproved par and yardage labels from the source illustrations are cropped out of every export.
