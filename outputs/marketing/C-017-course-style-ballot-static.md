# C-017 — Course style ballot static carousel

Status: Ready for Brand review; founder publication approval Pending

## Purpose

Create an awareness-stage Instagram carousel that invites a low-friction visual preference response without implying product availability or adding a gameplay claim. The ballot compares three founder-owned visual studies of the same course geometry and uses the official Flickline identity unchanged.

## Instagram-ready caption

Same course. Three visual studies. ⛳

A brings soft watercolor and field-guide texture. B turns up the color and contrast. C keeps the loose pencil lines of a notebook sketch.

Which one catches your eye—A, B, or C?

Pen Pad Golf is a printed tabletop golf game played with a pen. It is in development. See how to play.

CTA: Pick A, B, or C. Then see how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Carousel sequence

1. Brand-led cover — “Same course. Three visual studies.” with A/B/C specimen cards and the exact compact Flickline mark.
2. Study A — a close, crop-safe watercolor course view with a factual description of its visible paper texture, greens, and finish.
3. Studies B and C — a balanced comparison of the bright graphic and pencil-sketch treatments.
4. Ballot — three equally sized thumbnails, the exact primary Flickline lockup, A/B/C prompt, See how to play CTA, and visible development disclosure.

Every slide is 1080 × 1350 px. Critical copy and official identity remain inside feed crop-safe margins. Unapproved hole, par, and yardage labels from the source studies are outside every export.
