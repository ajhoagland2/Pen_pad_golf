# Pen Pad Golf — official logo production brief

Status: Original design direction for production; not a finished mark and not legally cleared.

## Direction: the Flickline monogram

Create a compact, custom **PPG** monogram whose three letterforms are joined by one rising negative-space channel. It should read first as a friendly, rhythmic mark and second as the initials. The upward channel suggests the game's repeated stand–flick–mark progression without drawing a pen, golf hole, course, or product diagram.

The identity should feel tactile, clever, calm, and lightly competitive. It must sit naturally beside the existing warm paper, watercolor course art, editorial typography, and plainspoken lines “A little golf. A lot of fun.” and “Stand it. Flick it. Mark it.” It should not look like a country-club crest, sports team, tournament, golf-equipment company, or tech app.

## Mark geometry

- Build on a 12-unit square. Keep the visible monogram approximately 10.5 units wide × 9 units high with a minimum 0.75-unit clear zone inside the artboard.
- Draw three custom modules from the same 1.5-unit stroke logic: two upright **P** forms followed by an open **G**. Use soft outside shoulders (about 0.75-unit radius), slightly squarer inside corners, and flat terminals.
- Let the modules overlap optically by roughly 0.4 unit so the initials behave as one mark, not three typed letters. Raise each successive counter by about 0.35 unit; the movement should be perceptible but not italic or speedy.
- Run one unbroken diagonal negative-space channel from the lower-left stem of the first P, through both P counters, and into the open counter of the G. Use a shallow 12–15° rise. The G's short inward terminal stops the channel and supplies the visual “landing.”
- Keep all apertures generous. At the minimum digital size, no surviving gap may be thinner than one-sixth of the mark's height.
- Do not place the mark in a shield, circle, oval, ball, seal, or patch. Its asymmetric silhouette and internal channel are the identifying features.
- Produce an optically corrected micro version rather than mechanically shrinking: open the P counters, shorten the G terminal, and widen the channel by about 12% below 32 px.

## Wordmark and lockups

- Pair the symbol with a custom-drawn title-case **Pen Pad Golf** wordmark. Preserve the warm editorial character of Playfair Display, but do not use an untouched font setting as the final trademark: redraw at least the P, a, d, G, and terminal f/l joins into a coherent proprietary wordmark.
- Favor moderate serif contrast, open counters, sturdy hairlines, and no swashes. Avoid a heritage-club, blackletter, collegiate, or luxury-script voice.
- Use Playfair Display Semibold only as a production starting point and for campaign headlines; retain DM Sans Medium/Semibold for descriptors, UI, and functional copy.
- Primary lockup: symbol left, wordmark right, vertically centered, with spacing equal to one P-counter width. Secondary lockup: symbol above a centered wordmark. The symbol may stand alone only after brand introduction or in constrained spaces.
- Never add “EST.”, a founding year, “club,” a location, a faux registration symbol, or the tagline inside the logo.

## Color

The master must work in one color before any color version is approved.

- Ink — `#193225`: primary symbol and wordmark on light grounds.
- Paper — `#F7F1E4`: preferred ground and reverse mark on dark green.
- Deep green — `#31543B`: alternate ground or digital brand field.
- Olive — `#667A47`: supporting field only; do not split the monogram into multicolor letters.
- Rust — `#9C3F2C`: optional single “landing” accent on the G terminal in large applications only.
- Gold — `#9B7319`: editorial accent, not a metallic logo treatment.

Provide ink-on-paper, paper-on-ink, and solid-black masters. Do not use gradients, watercolor fill inside the mark, drop shadows, bevels, faux embossing, chrome, or photographic clipping masks. Watercolor and paper texture may surround the logo but must not define its edges.

## Negative-space behavior

The diagonal channel is structural, not decoration. It must remain open, continuous, and visible in every master. It represents successive movement across a page while the G terminal acts as the final mark; it should not be embellished with dots, arrows, motion streaks, a ball, or a destination pin. If the channel closes in embroidery, stamping, or small-screen tests, use the corrected micro artwork—never outline the whole symbol to compensate.

Clear space around any lockup equals the height of the P counter. Minimum sizes: 24 px high for the micro symbol, 40 px for the standard symbol, and 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup. Confirm legibility in solid one-color print, reversed digital use, laser-printer output, and a 32 px circular crop.

## Instagram avatar

Use the micro symbol alone, centered on a solid Ink circle or square canvas with a Paper mark. Keep the mark within the central 68% of the square so Instagram's circular mask cannot clip it. At avatar size, omit the wordmark, tagline, rust accent, texture, border, and course imagery. Test at 110 px and 32 px; the channel must still read as one clean opening.

The existing watercolor profile-image concept is useful campaign art but is too detailed to serve as the official avatar. The website's current flag-in-oval icon is also transitional and must not be used as geometry for the new logo.

## Prohibited motifs and similarity guardrails

Do not use or trace: golf balls or dimple fields; tees; clubs; crossed equipment; flags or flagsticks; cups; greens; fairway silhouettes; scorecards; laurels; crowns; shields; animals; tournament rings; country-club seals; varsity lettering; map pins; pen nibs; literal upright or flicking pens; hands; page curls; or diagrams of the playing method. Do not adapt the silhouette, letter construction, palette arrangement, or negative-space trick of any known golf brand, sports mark, tournament identity, product illustration, or logo supplied only as inspiration. References may establish category distance, never serve as tracing or compositional templates.

## Production and approval

Deliver editable vector masters (SVG, PDF, EPS), outlined and live-type versions, RGB/CMYK/spot-color specifications, one-color and reverse variants, horizontal/stacked lockups, micro artwork, favicon exports, and 1024/320/110/32 px avatar tests. Include a one-page usage sheet showing clear space, minimum size, backgrounds, and misuse examples.

Before adoption, commission a professional trademark clearance search covering **Pen Pad Golf**, **PPG**, the custom monogram, and confusingly similar marks in relevant game, printed-goods, entertainment, and golf-related classes and markets. This brief is an originality direction, not legal clearance or a registrability opinion.
