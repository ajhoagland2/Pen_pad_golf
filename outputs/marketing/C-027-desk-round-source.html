Desk round / 01

Make room for a round.

A pen. A printed course. A little golf on the desk.

The desk becomes the clubhouse. The page becomes the course.

Desk round / start

Begin in the tee box.

Stand the pen upright. Flick toward the hole.

Desk round / continue

Move across the page.

Wherever the pen lands, mark the spot as one stroke.

Mark the landing · Continue from there

Desk round / finish

Follow the course to the flag.

Stand it at the new mark and flick again.

Each landing is one stroke.

Lowest score wins.
