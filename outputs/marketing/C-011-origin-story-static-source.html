From a blank page

What if a round could begin here?

A pen. A printed course. A new way to play.

The idea takes shape

A course appears on paper.

Watercolor fairways, paper texture, and one clear path to the flag.

Then the pen stands up

Now the round can begin.

Start in the tee box. Flick toward the hole.

Stand it. Flick it. Mark it.

Every landing is one stroke.

Mark the landing and keep playing. Lowest score wins.
