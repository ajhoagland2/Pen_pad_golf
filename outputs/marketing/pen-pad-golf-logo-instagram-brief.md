## What the identity needs to communicate

Pen Pad Golf is a printed tabletop golf game played with a pen. Its clearest promise is “A little golf. A lot of fun.” Its clearest mechanic is “Stand it. Flick it. Mark it.” The identity should feel tactile, clever, friendly, and grounded in golf—not like a golf simulator, betting product, country club, or children’s clip-art brand.

The current visual language is strong and should inform the new mark without being copied literally:

- warm printed paper and lightly imperfect texture;
- natural ink, green, olive, gold, and rust colors;
- watercolor course art with a blue pen and small red flag as recognizable accents;
- straightforward gameplay language and generous editorial layouts;
- Playfair Display for expressive headlines and DM Sans for functional information.

Representative course content confirms three useful visual modes: **Classic** is soft, tactile, and closest to the current brand; **Modern** is brighter and more graphic; **Sketch** communicates process and development. The logo should sit comfortably across all three, but Classic is the primary tonal reference.

## Recommended logo system

Commission a small system, not one overloaded badge:

1. **Primary emblem:** a compact, original symbol that combines the distinctive pen-play mechanic with one unmistakable golf cue. The strongest territory is a simplified upright or flicking pen plus a flag/hole or a marked path. Use no more than two principal shapes and one accent detail.
2. **Primary lockup:** emblem plus “Pen Pad Golf” wordmark. Keep the name intact; do not abbreviate the public-facing name to PPG.
3. **Horizontal lockup:** for website headers and corner signatures on static posts.
4. **One-color mark:** solid ink or reversed paper version that remains recognizable without watercolor, texture, gradients, or tiny internal detail.

The emblem should be drawn for small-screen recognition, not made by shrinking a scene. Do not put the hand, whole course, trees, water, bunker, notepad, pen, flag, and name into one badge. The existing website’s flag icon is a useful category cue but is too generic by itself; the pen mechanic must provide the ownable distinction.

## Required sizes and aspect ratios

Create all artwork from a high-resolution/vector master, then export the following static social files. The dimensions below are the working standard for this launch; recheck the live Instagram composer immediately before publication because crop behavior can change.

| Use | Required export | Ratio | Usage rule |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Instagram profile image | 1080 × 1080 PNG | 1:1 | Transparent or paper background; Instagram displays it through a circle. No tagline or small wordmark. |
| Feed logo reveal | 1080 × 1350 PNG/JPG | 4:5 | Preferred launch-post format and default for every carousel slide. |
| Square fallback | 1080 × 1080 PNG/JPG | 1:1 | Use only when a square placement is specifically required; do not make it the primary feed master. |
| Static Story | 1080 × 1920 PNG/JPG | 9:16 | Static image only. Do not repurpose this as a Reel or add animation/audio. |
| Wordmark/bug overlay | Transparent PNG at 1080 px-wide source scale | flexible | Supply ink, paper/reverse, and one-color variants for nondestructive placement on approved static imagery. |

Instagram currently presents feed portraits effectively at 4:5 while profile-grid thumbnails may show a centered 3:4 preview. Therefore every 1080 × 1350 cover must also read inside a centered **1012 × 1350** grid-safe window; keep critical matter at least **34 px** from the left and right crop lines, with a practical design margin of **68–80 px** from every outer edge.

### Profile-image crop and legibility rules

- Keep the complete emblem inside the central **760 × 760 px** area of the 1080 × 1080 master. This is a deliberately conservative circle-safe zone.
- Place no critical feature in the four corners. Preview the actual circular mask before approval.
- The emblem, not the full wordmark, is the default avatar. If the name cannot be read at 40 px, remove it rather than enlarging it into visual clutter.
- Test at 320, 110, 64, and 40 px. The pen and golf cue must still be distinguishable at 40 px.
- Maintain clear space around the emblem equal to at least the width of the pen barrel in the finished symbol.
- Provide a paper-background version and a reversed dark-green version; both must pass a grayscale and low-contrast-phone test.

### Feed, carousel, and Story safe areas

- On 1080 × 1350 posts, keep the logo bug 64–80 px from the canvas edge and within the centered 3:4 grid preview.
- Use a compact emblem at roughly 96–140 px wide or the horizontal lockup at roughly 220–320 px wide; it should sign the post, not compete with the hook.
- On the first carousel slide, center the hook and essential brand recognition so the profile thumbnail still works when cropped.
- On 1080 × 1920 Stories, keep the emblem and essential copy within a conservative center area: at least 90 px from the sides, 250 px from the top, and 380 px from the bottom. Confirm against the current Instagram UI before posting.
- Avoid placing the logo directly over the blue pen, red flag, hole, hand, landing marks, or course labels. Use a paper panel or a quiet area of the artwork.
- Minimum recommended on-image type at full 1080 px width: 52 px for headlines, 34 px for supporting copy, and 28 px only for short disclosures. Verify at phone size.
- Text embedded in images should meet at least 4.5:1 contrast against its immediate background. Texture must not reduce legibility.
