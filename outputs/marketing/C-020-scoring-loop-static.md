Status: Ready for Brand review; founder publication approval Pending

## Purpose

Create a saveable Instagram scoring-loop carousel that explains the approved landing-by-landing rhythm without adding a new rule, performance promise, availability claim, or implied result. ## Instagram-ready caption

One flick at a time. ⛳

Flick toward the hole. Mark the landing as one stroke. Stand the pen at that landing and take the next flick. Finish the hole, compare scores, and remember: lowest score wins.

Stand it. Flick it. Mark it.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. Save this scoring loop, then see how to play.

CTA: Save the scoring loop and see how to play.

## Alt text

Four-slide cream-paper Pen Pad Golf carousel styled as a vintage field scorecard. Each slide pairs the unchanged official compact Flickline mark with founder-owned watercolor course art. Slide 1 introduces the landing-by-landing rhythm. Slide 2 traces a flick and marks the first landing as one stroke. Slide 3 continues from the marked landing. Slide 4 circles the flag and compares sample scores to state that lowest score wins. Every slide says the game is in development.
