# C-014 — The route is yours static carousel

Status: Revised after BRD-031; Brand review Pending; not approved for publication

## Purpose

Create a consideration-stage Instagram carousel that frames each printed hole as a simple route from tee box to flag. The package uses only approved gameplay and scoring facts, while the hook invites the viewer to picture the path without promising a specific result or product availability.

## Instagram-ready caption

The route is yours. ⛳

Start in the tee box. Stand the pen upright, flick toward the hole, and mark where it lands as one stroke. Continue from the new mark until you reach the hole.

Find the flag. Count the strokes. Lowest score wins.

Pen Pad Golf is in development. See how to play.

CTA: See how to play.

Hashtags: #PenPadGolf #TabletopGames #GolfGames #GameInDevelopment

## Carousel sequence

1. Route ledger cover — “The route is yours.” A light editorial field-note layout frames one founder-owned hole between “Tee box” and “Flag,” with the exact compact Flickline mark.
2. Start rule — “Stand the pen upright.” One founder-owned hole sits beside a deep-green rule panel; the full deterministic upright navy pen and its shadow are contained inside the printed tee box.
3. Landing rule — “Leave a mark.” One founder-owned hole carries a single navy landing dot fully on the broad playable fairway and a “Your next start” callout, followed by the literal continuation rule. There is no numbered route or multi-landing path.
4. Scorecard close — “Lowest score wins.” One course ribbon leads into a paper scorecard with the exact primary Flickline lockup, CTA, and visible development disclosure.

Every slide is 1080 × 1350 px. Critical copy and official identity remain inside feed crop-safe margins.
