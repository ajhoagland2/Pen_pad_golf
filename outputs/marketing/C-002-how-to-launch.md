## Approved visual treatment

- Display type: Playfair Display, weight 600, for the opening hook and end-card promise.
- Functional type: DM Sans, weight 600–700, for captions, gameplay text, CTA, and disclosure.
- Background and text: paper `#F7F1E4` with ink `#193225`.
- Supporting panels and rules: green `#31543B` and olive `#667A47`.
- Restrained accents only: gold `#9B7319` and rust `#9C3F2C`.
- Apply the webpage’s subtle printed-paper texture behind text panels; do not add glossy gradients, neon color, or modern gaming effects.
- Use short hard cuts and restrained push-ins. Do not use whip pans, artificial camera shake, speed ramps, lens flares, or simulated ball-flight graphics.
- Keep overlays away from the pen, hand, flag, course path, landing point, and scoring mark.

## Real-product constraints

- The physical product, printed course, pen action, hand action, cover art, and score marks must come from the approved source assets.
- Do not redraw, recolor, replace, extend, animate, or fabricate the product art.
- Do not synthesize a pen flick, landing, hand, course, packaging, or gameplay result.
- Do not present generated footage as real product footage. If a non-product background treatment is generated, label it as a concept and keep it visually separate from the product.
- Do not add price, availability, launch-date, shipping, durability, age-range, testimonial, or performance claims.
